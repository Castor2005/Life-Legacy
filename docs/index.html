🌟 Starting out

✨ Random life drops you anywhere in history with rolled traits and family. 🧬 Custom life lets you design everything — but set your DOB first: country, region & city unlock from it and are era-accurate (no modern nations before they existed). Then pick a background, up to 3 traits + 1 congenital ★, attribute points, and a life ambition. Quick starts: 🌱 Peasant (poor, high karma), 👑 Royal (rich, prestige-loaded), 🎲 Chaos (nemesis, crime flag).

⏳ The core loop

Press ⏳ Age + (or Space) to live a year — money, school, jobs, family and health update automatically. ▶ Auto plays for you. Between birthdays use the tabs: 🎯 Activities, 👥 Social, 👪 Family, 🎓 Education, 💰 Money, 🏥 Health, 👑 Rule, 🌍 World. Actions stay hidden until age-appropriate (babies nap and do tummy time; weddings, jobs and college surface in your teens). Click the 👑 logo or Life & Legacy title anytime to jump to the front page — your living sim autosaves first.

🎯 Focus

Pick a 🎯 Focus (era- and background-flavoured, or dictate your own — fate judges it) to shape yearly bonuses: grades, money, fame, health, love and more. Switch anytime from the plaque under your character sheet.

🎓 School & careers

Study hard early — low grades block college, 90+ grades win a scholarship. Finish high school to unlock the Higher-education block: college plus 13 majors (Medicine, Engineering, Law, Arts…), then grad school. Majors gate the best of 40+ careers. Jobs, businesses, houses, cars and stocks live under 💰 Money and 🌍 World.

👪 Family & dynasty

Parents, siblings and grandparents live their own lives — jobs, marriages, babies, deaths. Marry, have kids, name an heir, write a will. The 🌳 Dynasty button opens your family tree (rooted at the earliest known generation) plus prestige, renown and heir selection — adult heirs can continue the run after you die.

🎲 Events & Director

Random events pop up — choices shape stats, money, prestige and story, and traits unlock special options. On any event you can ✏️ type a custom response (AI fate decides) or 🌟 invent a custom event. 🎬 Director Mode (top bar) stages ANYTHING you type with cinematic art, applies grounded mechanics with the exact effects shown on each choice, plus a free-type box — 🎲 Random scene invents one for you. Crime can land you in jail — behave (or break out). The game master never refuses or lectures; consequences play out in-world.

💀 Death & legacy

Keep ❤️ Health above zero and manage 😰 Stress. Health falls every year — faster as you age (under 30: −1/yr, 30s–40s: −2, 50s–60s: −4, 70+: −6; high fitness slows it, stress and debt speed it). Happiness drifts with love, health, money and stress. Crime builds a 🌙 record that blocks trusted jobs and elections, and jail always costs your job — karma opens doors, or closes them. Death brings a ⭐ legacy score, a ribbon (Rich, Superstar, Villain, Saintly…) and an AI obituary. Build ⭐ Fame, 👑 Prestige and ☯️ Karma while alive — they all feed the score.

🤖 AI engine & Google Search Grounding

The biographer, Director Mode, World Timeline divergence analysis, chats, and real-world grounding run first through server-side Gemini when hosted (with live Google Search facts and clickable citations), otherwise the Perchance built-in engine or your own endpoint below — with a deterministic local fallback if you go offline.

💾 Saves

Autosave fires every year and action — the front page's Last-soul card always offers your latest life. Pin favourites into 7 slots, download them as files, or import one. Click the 👑 logo or title anytime — your sim autosaves first. UI is translated (EN/ES/FR/PT/RU/HI/中文) with 5 visual themes; 🎨 style, 🌍 language and ❓ help live in the top bar (··· on phones); life stories are told in English.
